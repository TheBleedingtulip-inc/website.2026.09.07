The Bleeding Tulip

A nonprofit community resource and empowerment organization.

Our Mission

We help people move from isolation and crisis toward stability, connection, independence, and opportunity through community support, resource navigation, and practical services.

What We Do

  • Peer support circles and community connection
  • Resource navigation and local referrals
  • Digital access support (computers, internet, and phone charging)
  • Employment and education readiness support
  • Partnership-based outreach with local organizations

Get Involved

We welcome volunteers, community partners, and supporters who want to help build a stronger and more connected community.
