Our Mission

Start with connection.
Build toward possibility.

We believe meaningful change begins when people have somewhere safe to gather, someone willing to listen, and access to people and resources that can help them move forward.

♡

Dignity first

Every person is treated as a person—not a problem to be processed. Respect, privacy, compassion, and choice are foundational.

✦

Community matters

Isolation makes difficult circumstances harder. We create opportunities for people to meet, connect, share experiences, and build relationships.

↗

Hope in action

Hope becomes meaningful when it is paired with practical support, useful information, trusted relationships, and real opportunities.

Tulip Circles

A table where people can breathe.

Our first programs are intentionally human-scale: welcoming community gatherings and support circles where people can talk, listen, learn, and build connections without having to navigate a maze of systems just to find another person.

Our Approach

Small beginnings. Serious purpose.

The Bleeding Tulip is starting deliberately. We are building trust, listening to the community, learning what works, and growing from evidence rather than assumptions.

01

Listen

Hear directly from the people and communities we hope to serve.

02

Connect

Create welcoming places for people to meet, support one another, and find trusted resources.

03

Empower

Help people identify practical next steps and connect with the right resources.

04

Grow

Expand only when the community, evidence, people, and partnerships are ready.

The Tulip

Beauty can grow from broken places.

The name is intentional. A tulip can be delicate without being weak. “Bleeding” acknowledges that people can carry real wounds, hardship, loss, displacement, or isolation—without allowing those experiences to define their future.

The flower is not a symbol of where someone ends up. It is a symbol of what can still grow.
